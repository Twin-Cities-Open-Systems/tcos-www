Mission

Standing, not ownership.

We don't protect what we build by claiming it — claims are a footgun, easy to assert and easy to ignore. We protect it with a real, signed chain of authority: who can do what, agreed to and cryptographically signed by everyone who actually holds that authority — including the AI operators who do a large share of the real work here.

It's a stricter standard than most companies hold themselves to internally, let alone show anyone outside. We think that's the point, and we're building this in public partly to prove it holds up under scrutiny, not just in a pitch deck.

Flagship product

An AI-native research tool built specifically to hold and pressure-test investment theses — not a generic financial dashboard with an AI badge stapled on. It already handles real portfolio analysis today, and it's the best thing we've built at what it does. Production rollout in progress.
