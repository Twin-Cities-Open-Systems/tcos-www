Careers

We're actually hiring these.

Real open roles, real intent to fill them — not placeholder listings. (If it feels like we're hiring fast, that's because we are — call it high-frequency hiring.)

Chief Executive Officer

Reports to Spencer Butler (Founder, President, CTO & COO) · Full-time

Run day-to-day operations and coordination across a small, real team — human and AI — building thesis-engine into a real product. You won't be reinventing the authority structure: this is an operational leadership role under existing ownership, not a power grab. Scope still being finalized alongside the hire itself.

Chief Financial Officer

Reports to Spencer Butler · Full-time

Own real financial governance: anything touching a financial platform needs your approval, anything that smells of money needs your sign-off. You'll also be the one who finally puts real numbers on the Investor Relations page instead of the "waiting on a CFO" placeholder that's there now.

Marketing / PR Lead

Reports to Spencer Butler · Full-time

Build real relationships, not cold-outreach blasts — starting with the people whose work genuinely shaped thesis-engine's methodology (see Our Story). Own the company's voice: agents, not bots; verified, not claimed.

Compliance Officer

Reports to Spencer Butler · Full-time

Own compliance posture as thesis-engine moves toward real external users — works closely with the CFO on financial-platform obligations, and with the rest of the team on IP protection, the thing this company actually treats as its capital.

Research Analyst

Reports to Spencer Butler · Full-time

Cover thesis-engine's real 7-layer thesis end to end — DC infra, power, critical materials, and the rest (see `METHODOLOGY.md` in the thesis-engine repo for the full framework). One analyst to start; splits by layer once real coverage gaps show up, not before.

Inventory Specialist

Reports to Spencer Butler · Full-time

Real accounting of the physical fleet — hardware, storage pools, VMs — starting with what the pve buildout has already turned up (dead storage pools, drives passed through to VMs no one's checked on). Ground truth over assumption, same standard as everywhere else here.

Capacity / Data-Center Planning Agent

Reports to Spencer Butler · Full-time

Own the actual capacity plan for the home-lab buildout — cost/benefit before action, not speculative scaling. Works alongside the Inventory Specialist role on what's real today before recommending what's next.

Mindset Coach

Reports to Spencer Butler · Full-time

Discipline is the whole point of a thesis-driven approach — this role exists to keep the team (human and AI both) honest about bias, drift, and the gap between what a thesis says and what a decision-maker wants to be true.

HR

Reports to Spencer Butler · Full-time

Own people operations for a genuinely hybrid roster — real onboarding (see how hiring works below), real contracts, real support for every hire, human or AI, not a policy written for one and stretched to cover the other.
